Welcome to my mod. It is based on Hogwarts legacy. 

To play it make a mod folder and put in my pak file. THen in quak4 go to mod section and click on the mod.

You spawn in with 10 weapons which are meant to be 10 spells. 

There is a plant and potion crafting mechanic. You get seeds by killing monsters. Everything is done through commands for now.

Commands:
list_allamoo  --> shows you how many seeds, plants, potions you have
plant  --> plant the seed, removes a seed and gives you a plant
craft --> craft potions using plants
drink --> drink potion and get their effects
give ammo --> gives you max of everything to test out all features.
